Doz and CoCare Trips: one-page summary

A phone app for drivers and an office view that calculates each trip. Demo, with invented data.

What the app does

  • Driver, on a phone: sees the next trip, taps arrived, confirms the contract, starts, records any waiting, ends, and enters the odometer. Every step is confirmed on screen.
  • Office: sees what needs attention first, checks each trip's mileage against the broker's authorised miles, approves trips for billing, adds drivers and imports trips.
  • The calculation: flat fee + (approved miles × rate per mile). Contract A is $13.50 + $1.60 a mile and Contract B is $15.00 + $1.75 a mile. Both are the owner's figures and are unconfirmed. The result is the amount charged, not profit.
  • Not built yet: real login, saved data, live GPS, invoicing, driver pay. Waiting time is recorded but not charged until a rule is confirmed.

The HIPAA workaround: calculation only

HIPAA depends on who you are in the contract and what data you hold, not on whether the app "does care". So the app can be set to hold only what the calculation needs.

  • Stored: trip ID, date, time, contract, driver, odometer, authorised miles.
  • Not stored: passenger names or references, pickup and destination addresses, notes.
  • The setting is on the Contracts page. It is on by default in the demo, and can be switched to full details to show the client both.

The catch: drivers need the addresses

In calculation-only mode the driver gets the pickup and destination from the broker's own system or manifest, not from this app. That is one extra step for the driver. If the addresses must be inside this app, there are two options:

  • Keep calculation only and let the broker's system stay the source of addresses. Cheapest.
  • Store addresses with HIPAA-capable hosting and a signed agreement. About 25 times the hosting cost (below), and it needs the broker's go-ahead.

Whether storing addresses brings HIPAA in is unconfirmed. It depends on the broker contract and is a question for the broker or a healthcare lawyer. This page is not legal advice.

What must be confirmed before real use

  • Does the broker or payer require a HIPAA business associate agreement, and what trip data may you store?
  • Is the service NEMT vans only, or also ambulance or stretcher transport? That changes the answer.
  • Are the contract rates revenue, driver pay, or both? Is waiting time paid?
  • How many trips a month, and how many drivers? Everything above assumes about 10 drivers.
  • The demo has no back end yet. Login, saved data and access rules must be built and checked before real trips go in.
